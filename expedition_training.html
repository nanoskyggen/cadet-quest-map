Test your mastery of all eleven outdoor expedition training modules. The Arena presents randomized questions drawn from every module — kit packing, weather reading, campsite selection, shelter building, fire safety, water purification, knots, navigation, leave-no-trace, wildlife safety, and emergency response. Answer correctly to build your streak and confirm your expedition fundamentals. Complete a 10-question round with 8+ correct to earn the Expedition Arena badge.
