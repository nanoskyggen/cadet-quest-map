▸ Situation Reports

SITREP Filing

A Situation Report (SITREP) is how cadet musicians communicate training status to their bandmaster. File a SITREP after each module to log what you learned, any instrument care issues identified, and your readiness for the next level.

▸ Proficiency Progression

Music Proficiency Ladder

As you complete training modules and earn XP, you advance through the cadet music proficiency levels. Each level corresponds to the Canadian Cadet Music Program standards — from Basic through Level 5. Higher levels unlock leadership roles such as Drum Major or Pipe Major.

▸ Learning Library

Music Knowledge Base

Reference materials covering the theory behind each training module — music theory, instrument care, band drill, marches and calls, and proficiency standards. Read these before or after modules to deepen your understanding. Reading articles also earns a small XP bonus.

▸ Ear Training Studio

Ear Lab

Listen to notes played by different cadet band instruments, then place them on the musical staff. Train your ear to identify pitch and learn how notes map to the five-line staff. Select an instrument voice, play a note, and drag it onto the staff to build a melody.

🎺 Instrument Voice

Choose an instrument timbre. Each voice uses a distinct synthesis model to approximate the real instrument's sound.

🎹 Note Palette

Click any note to hear it played by the selected instrument. The note will be staged for placement on the staff.

🎼 Musical Staff

Click a position on the staff to place the staged note. Build a melody by placing several notes left-to-right. Press Play Melody to hear the full sequence.

🎵 On-Screen Keyboard

Play notes directly on the keyboard. Each key is labelled with its note name. Click to hear it with the selected instrument voice.

▸ Composition Studio

Composer

Create your own music from scratch. Pick the instruments you want from the full band, lay notes into the sequencer grid, set the tempo, and press play to hear your composition. Wind, brass, and melodic instruments use real recorded samples for authentic sound — experiment freely and build your own cadet march, fanfare, or melody.

🎺 Select Your Instruments

Tap an instrument to add it to your composition

🎹 Sequencer Grid

Each selected instrument appears below as its own grid block. Click a block's coloured header to make it active (editable), then click cells to toggle notes. Active notes are bright; other tracks show faded ghosts so you can see the whole arrangement. Press Play to hear all instruments together.
