▸ Bridge Watch Simulator

Kingston-Class MCDV — Bridge

Step onto the bridge of a Kingston-Class Maritime Coastal Defence Vessel. Instead of answering questions, you will operate the ship — set the heading on the helm, ring the engine telegraph, scan the lookout sectors, read the radar, toggle navigation lights, work the anchor, and communicate on VHF. Each watch presents a real-world scenario with events to resolve using the right station at the right time.
