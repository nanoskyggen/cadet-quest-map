⚠️ Hazard Assessment

Marksmanship hazards cadets must remain aware of on the firing point. Threat levels update as you progress.

SAFECAUTIONHIGH
🫁
Breath Timing Fault
Firing during inhalation or at full lung capacity — causes vertical shot displacement and inconsistent group height.
HIGH
👆
Trigger Jerk
Pulling or jerking the trigger instead of a smooth squeeze — the most common cause of horizontal shot displacement.
HIGH
👁️
Poor Sight Alignment
Front sight not centred in rear aperture — even a small misalignment compounds downrange into large misses.
MED
🧭
Wrong Natural Point of Aim
Position not naturally pointing at the target — muscle tension creeps in to hold the rifle on aim, degrading the hold.
MED

Five Range Qualification Modes

👁️
Sight Alignment — Align the front sight in the rear aperture and centre it on the target. Drag to correct the sight picture.
🫁
Breath Control — Watch the breathing cycle and fire during the natural respiratory pause at the end of exhale.
👆
Trigger Squeeze — Apply smooth, steady pressure to the trigger. No jerking — achieve the surprise break.
🧭
Natural Point of Aim — Adjust your body position so the rifle points naturally at the target without muscle tension.
🎯
Shot Grouping — Fire a five-shot string, then diagnose the group pattern and zero your sights.

Press Start Qualification to begin. Modes are called one at a time in random order.
