Five Emergency Response Modes

🩺
Primary Survey (DRABC) — Run the Danger, Response, Airway, Breathing, Circulation sequence on a simulated casualty. Make the correct assessment at each stage.
🫀
CPR Rhythm — Perform chest compressions at the correct tempo (100–120 per minute). Keep the beat steady for 30 compressions.
🩸
Bleeding Control — Apply treatment steps in the correct order to control severe bleeding before the casualty deteriorates.
😮‍💨
Choking Response — Assess the choking casualty and choose the correct technique: back blows, abdominal thrusts, or when to call for help.
🛌
Recovery Position — Place an unconscious breathing casualty into the recovery position step by step to protect their airway.
