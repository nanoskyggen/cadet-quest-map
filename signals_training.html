Your signals instructor has cleared you to begin the Advanced Signals & Comms training rotation. Work through each performance objective in sequence. Each mission is a hands-on signalling simulation — you will use flares, signal mirrors, whistles, ground-to-air code sheets, signal fire construction guides, radio net procedure cards, and authentication tables just like a real cadet coordinating a search-and-rescue response during a field exercise.

⚠️ Comms Hazard Assessment

Signalling and communications hazards cadets must remain aware of in the field. Threat levels update as you progress.

SAFECAUTIONHIGH
🔥
Uncontrolled Signal Fire
Lighting a signal fire without clearing the area or notifying authorities can start a forest fire and endanger the cadet and rescuers.
HIGH
📡
Radio Dead Zone
Terrain and elevation can block radio signals. Failing to move to higher ground leaves the cadet unable to call for help.
HIGH
🪞
Misaimed Signal Mirror
Flashing the mirror at the wrong angle or failing to sight along the arm means rescuers never see the reflection.
MED
🔋
Battery Depletion
Leaving a radio or cell phone on drains the battery. In an emergency, turn off the device between check-in times to conserve power.
LOW
