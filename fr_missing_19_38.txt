=== INDEX 19 ===
A Situation Report (SITREP) is how cadets communicate survival status to their instructor. File a SITREP after each mission to log what you learned and any survival errors you identified in the field.

=== INDEX 20 ===
Correct! A lean-to is an IMPROVISED shelter — you build it by leaning branches and materials against a ridgepole or between trees. Other improvised shelters include bough shelters, fallen trunk shelters, snow caves, and tepees.

=== INDEX 21 ===
Correct! All water from natural sources must be purified before drinking, no matter how clear it looks. Boiling for at least 1 minute (3+ at altitude) or using purification tablets kills harmful bacteria, viruses, and parasites like Giardia.

=== INDEX 22 ===
Correct! Cirrus clouds (high, thin, wispy, horse-tail appearance) often indicate fair weather at present, but when they increase and spread across the sky, a weather change — often rain or a front — is likely within 12-24 hours.

=== INDEX 23 ===
Correct! Cumulonimbus clouds — tall, towering clouds with an anvil-shaped top — indicate thunderstorms are approaching. Prepare your shelter, secure your fire area, and be ready for heavy rain, lightning, and strong winds.

=== INDEX 24 ===
Correct! Elevation and drainage are critical. Cold air sinks into low areas and valleys at night, and water pools in depressions. An elevated, well-drained site prevents hypothermia from ground cold and dampness.

=== INDEX 25 ===
Correct! Fear is not one of the seven enemies. The seven enemies are: Pain, Cold, Thirst, Hunger, Fatigue, Boredom, and Loneliness. Fear is related but it falls under the psychology of survival, not the seven enemies list.

=== INDEX 26 ===
Correct! Food is the lowest survival priority. The rule of three says you can survive 3 weeks without food, compared to 3 days without water and 3 hours without shelter in extreme conditions. Focus on shelter and water first.

=== INDEX 27 ===
Correct! General warning signs of poisonous plants include: milky sap, bitter or soapy taste, almond scent (cyanide), spines or thorns, and plants with umbrella-shaped flower clusters. These are not absolute rules but strong caution signals.

=== INDEX 28 ===
Correct! Ground-to-air signal symbols should be 1 metre wide and 8 metres long, with individual symbols placed 3 metres apart. This size ensures visibility from searching aircraft. Symbols include V (require assistance), X (require medical assistance), and arrows for direction.

=== INDEX 29 ===
Correct! Improvised filtration removes visible sediment and particles but does not kill microorganisms. You must ALWAYS follow filtration with boiling or chemical purification tablets to make the water truly safe to drink.

=== INDEX 30 ===
Correct! Nimbostratus clouds — dark grey, flat, featureless layers covering the sky — bring continuous, steady rain that can last for hours. This is different from the brief heavy downpours of thunderstorms. Prepare for prolonged wet conditions.

=== INDEX 31 ===
Correct! Once your survival basics are secured, focus on signalling. Stay at your shelter (it provides protection and is a landmark), prepare three fires for aerial distress, lay out ground-to-air signal symbols, and keep a signal mirror and whistle ready. Multiple signal methods increase your chances of being spotted by rescuers.

=== INDEX 32 ===
Correct! The best way to counter boredom and loneliness is to stay busy — build shelters, improve your camp, maintain fire, keep a routine, and maintain hope. An active mind and body resist psychological collapse.

=== INDEX 33 ===
Correct! The golden rule of foraging is absolute: if you cannot identify a plant with 100% certainty, do not eat it. Food is the lowest survival priority (you can survive weeks without it), so the risk of poisoning is never worth taking.

=== INDEX 34 ===
Correct! The proper extinguishing procedure is: 1) Douse thoroughly with water, 2) Stir the ashes to expose all hot spots, 3) Feel for residual heat with your hand, 4) Fill the pit with earth. Only when it is cold to the touch is it safe to leave.

=== INDEX 35 ===
Correct! The universal distress signal is THREE of anything: three whistle blasts, three mirror flashes, three gunshots, or three fires. This pattern is internationally recognized as a distress call. Three evenly spaced fires in a triangle is a standard aerial distress signal.

=== INDEX 36 ===
Correct! The universal edibility test begins by separating the plant into parts (leaves, stems, roots, flowers), then smelling each part for almond or acidic odors which can indicate cyanide or acid. If it smells of almonds, do not eat it. Further steps include testing on skin, then lips, tongue, and finally swallowing a small amount with an 8-hour wait.

=== INDEX 37 ===
Correct! Three fires arranged in a triangle, spaced 35 metres apart, is the internationally recognized aerial distress signal. This pattern is clearly visible from aircraft and distinguishes a distress signal from a campfire or natural fire.

=== INDEX 38 ===
Correct! When birds fly low and insects become less active, it often signals dropping air pressure and approaching bad weather. Many animals sense pressure changes before storms and alter their behavior accordingly.
