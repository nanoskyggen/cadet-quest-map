How to Play — Patrol Range

Mission

You command a four-cadet section on patrol. Enemy sentries patrol the terrain and will detect any cadet who breaks the principles of why things are seen. Your task is to move the section to each objective undetected using silent field signals.

How to Direct a Cadet

1. Click a friendly contact (green dot) on the tactical map to select it. Its data block appears in the control panel.

2. Build a field signal by clicking chips in order: callsign → hand signal → direction/detail.

3. Send the signal. The cadet follows your order and moves across the terrain.

Movement Techniques

• Leopard Crawl — slowest, lowest profile, hardest to detect. Use in open ground.

• Monkey Run — medium speed, crouched. Good for partial cover.

• Ghost Walk — fast, upright. Only safe in dead ground or behind solid cover.

• Halt — freeze in place. Drops your detection risk to zero while still.

Field Signals

• Advance + direction — move the cadet in that compass direction.

• Halt — stop and freeze; concealment improves while still.

• Take Cover — dash to nearest cover/concealment marker.

• Observe — hold position and scan; can spot nearby sentries.

Why Things Are Seen

Sentries detect cadets who show shape (silhouette against sky), shine (reflective gear), shadow, silhouette, movement, spacing (regular intervals look unnatural), or surface (contrasting colour). Moving fast in the open is the quickest way to be detected. Use cover, move slowly, and choose the right technique for the ground.

Scoring

• +50 per cadet reaching an objective undetected.

• +10 per successful observation of a sentry.

• −20 per detection (cadet compromised).

• Complete all four cadets to advance the wave and spawn a harder patrol.
