How to Play — Air Traffic Control

Mission

You are the duty controller. Inbound aircraft appear on the radar scope with call signs built from the ICAO phonetic alphabet (e.g. ALPHA-7, BRAVO-3). Your job is to talk each one down safely.

How to Direct an Aircraft

1. Click a radar contact (green dot) to select it. Its data block appears in the control panel.

2. Build a radio transmission by clicking chips in order: call sign → "this is Tower," → instruction → detail.

3. Transmit. If the call sign and instruction are correct, the aircraft follows your order and heads for the runway.

Instructions

• Cleared to land — direct the aircraft to its matched runway for landing. Worth the most points.

• Cleared for runway — sends the aircraft to the runway threshold to join the approach.

• Hold — places the aircraft in a holding pattern. Use when runways are busy.

• Turn heading — steer the aircraft to a compass heading (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW).

Scoring

• Clean landing: +100 • Successful runway join: +40 • Hold placed: +15

• Wrong call sign: −20 • Aircraft leaves airspace uncontrolled: −30 • Collision: −50

Watch Out

Each wave adds more aircraft and they arrive faster. Aircraft with low fuel blink red — prioritise them. If two aircraft get too close at similar altitude, a collision alert fires. Keep them separated!
