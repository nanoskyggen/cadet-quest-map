Current Operation: Bushcraft & Survival

Your survival instructor has cleared you to begin the Bushcraft & Survival training rotation. Work through each performance objective in sequence. Each mission is a hands-on survival simulation — you will apply the S.T.O.P. procedure, build emergency shelters, light and maintain fires, purify water, identify safe forage, and signal for rescue just like a real cadet operating in the wilderness during a survival exercise.

▸ Survival Trail

Survival Trail — Interactive Wilderness Simulator

You are stranded alone in the wilderness with nightfall approaching. The Survival Trail is a multi-stage interactive scenario that tests everything you have learned. Make the right decisions at each stage — S.T.O.P., build shelter, light a fire, purify water, and signal for rescue. Every choice affects your survival condition meters. Complete all stages successfully to earn the Survival Trail badge. Toggle on the campfire music for an immersive wilderness atmosphere.
