▸ Command Overview

Parade Drill Command

Welcome to Operation Cerberus. This ceremonial drill program covers the advanced parade drill syllabus from the Canadian Armed Forces Manual of Drill and Ceremonial (A-DH-201-000/PT-000, known as "the 201"). You will build on basic foot drill and progress to arms drill with the drill rifle — shoulder arms, present arms, port arms, and the salute at the shoulder — then ceremonial rifle movements such as recover arms and reverse arms. The course culminates in colour party drill (carry, slope, let fly, catch, and dip) and sequencing a full parade compliment. Complete training modules to earn XP, climb the rank ladder, and file your SITREPs.

How to Play — Parade Ground

Mission

You are on the parade ground executing ceremonial drill per the 201 manual (A-DH-201-000/PT-000). The parade commander calls each command. You must select the correct movement — arms drill with the drill rifle, or colour party drill with the colour. Watch the animated squad respond in real time and keep the cadence.

How to Respond

1. The commander calls a command in two parts — the cautionary (e.g. "Squad") then the executive (e.g. "PRESENT ARMS").

2. Click the correct movement button from the Arms Drill or Colour Party grid.

3. The animated cadet squad and colour party execute the movement on screen.

4. Respond quickly for a timing bonus — faster responses earn more XP.

Arms Drill — Rifle Positions

• Shoulder Arms — rifle butt on the shoulder, the carrying position.

• Present Arms — rifle brought to the vertical in front of the body; the ceremonial salute with the rifle.

• Port Arms — rifle held diagonally across the body; a tactical carry and position of readiness.

• Salute at the Shoulder — salute given while the rifle remains at the shoulder.

• Recover Arms — rifle brought vertical in front of the body, a ceremonial position.

• Reverse Arms — rifle reversed (inverted) as a mark of respect, used in funeral drill.

Colour Party Drill — Flag Positions

• Carry — the colour held upright at the carry, the normal position.

• Slope — the colour pike sloped over the shoulder.

• Let Fly — the colour allowed to fly free from the pike, done on the first movement of the present arms during a salute.

• Catch — the colour caught and gathered back onto the pike, done on the command to shoulder arms.

• Dip — the colour lowered in salute, used for a Royal Salute to entitled dignitaries.

General Salute Compliment

Click General Salute to trigger a full compliment sequence. The commander orders the general salute and you must execute each movement in the correct 201 sequence: Present Arms → Let Fly the Colour → Catch the Colour → Shoulder Arms. Complete all four movements correctly for a large bonus.

Scoring

• Each correct response earns XP based on the command difficulty, plus a timing bonus (up to +50% for very fast responses).

• Wrong responses reset your streak and show the correct movement.

• Completing a full General Salute compliment awards a +25 XP sequence bonus.
