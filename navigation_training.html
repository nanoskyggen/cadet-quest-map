How to Play — Trail Navigator

Mission

You are a cadet navigator. Starting at HQ, you must navigate across the topographic map to the campsite, passing through three checkpoints along the way. At each decision point, you choose a cardinal direction to move. Read the map carefully — contour lines, water symbols, and terrain features all matter.

How to Navigate

1. Read the Navigation Target panel — it shows your next checkpoint, its description, and its grid reference.

2. Study the topographic map. Find your current position (the pulsing amber dot) and the green checkpoint marker.

3. Click a cardinal direction button (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) to move in that heading.

4. Your route is drawn on the map as a trail line. The map updates after each move.

Reading the Map

• Contour lines show elevation. Close lines mean steep ground — avoid cliff bands. Widely spaced lines mean gentle slopes.

• Blue lines are water features — streams and rivers. Avoid unnecessary crossings.

• Green areas are forest cover — good for handrail navigation along treelines.

• Red zones are restricted or hazardous areas — never enter them.

• Grid lines divide the map into squares. Use the grid reference to locate your checkpoint precisely.

Scoring

• +30 for reaching each checkpoint.

• +50 for reaching the campsite.

• +10 bonus for an efficient route (fewer steps to reach the checkpoint).

• −10 for each wrong direction (moving away from the checkpoint or into a hazard).

• Complete all checkpoints and reach the campsite to advance the wave — a new, harder route appears.

Terrain Hint

Stuck? Click Terrain Hint for a clue about the direction of your next checkpoint. Using hints reduces your efficiency bonus but keeps you moving.
