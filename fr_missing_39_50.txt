The correct filter order (bottom to top) is: cloth/grass (catches fines) → fine sand → crushed charcoal (removes chemicals and odors) → fine sand → pour water through. Remember: this filters sediment only — you must still boil or chemically treat the water to kill pathogens.

=== INDEX 45 ===
The correct fuel order is: Tinder (catches spark) → Kindling (catches from tinder) → Wood Fuel (sustains flame) → Bulk Firewood (long-lasting heat). Each stage must be properly alight before adding the next, or you will smother the fire.

The correct order for building a debris shelter is: 1) Find a ridgepole, 2) Prop one end elevated, 3) Lean rib branches to form the A-frame, 4) Pile insulating debris over the frame. The debris layer should be at least 30cm thick for adequate insulation.

The five elements of survival in priority order are: Attitude, Shelter, Water, Fire, Food. Your mental attitude is the most critical — without the will to survive, nothing else matters. Shelter and water come before fire and food because exposure and dehydration kill faster than hunger.
