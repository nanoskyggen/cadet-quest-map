Current Operation: First-Year Drill Rotation

Your drill instructor has cleared you to begin the first-year cadet drill rotation. Work through each performance objective in sequence, from the position of attention through full parade procedures. Each mission is a hands-on drill simulation — you will use the foot diagram, turn compass, cadence metronome, squad alignment chart, saluting pace tracker, parade formation diagram, and rank knowledge tools just like a real cadet on the square.

⚠️ Hazard Assessment

Drill and parade hazards cadets must remain aware of on the square. Threat levels update as you progress.

SAFECAUTIONHIGH
🦶
Incorrect Foot Position
Heels not together, feet not at 30°, or weight unevenly distributed — leads to poor balance and sloppy drill.
MED
🧭
Turn Direction Error
Pivoting on the wrong heel or toe, or turning the wrong way — breaks squad alignment and timing.
MED
⏱️
Cadence Break
Marching out of step with the squad — the most visible drill fault and hardest to correct mid-march.
HIGH
🫡
Saluting Timing Fault
Salute too early, too late, or incorrect pace count — a courtesy failure noticed by every officer on parade.
