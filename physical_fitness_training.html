Fitness Command

Welcome to the Physical Fitness training program. This station covers the first-year Physical Fitness syllabus: safe warm-up and cool-down routines, the importance of diet and hydration, building healthy habits through movement and pacing, and setting personal improvement goals. You will learn to design a safe three-part training plan, explain the role of nutrition and water in performance, demonstrate proper exercise form, and understand how teamwork supports fitness activities. Five Fitness Circuit Modes

🔥
Warm-Up Sequence — Arrange a dynamic warm-up in the correct order: pulse-raiser, dynamic stretches, mobility work, and sport-specific drills. Get the sequence right to prepare safely.
🏃
Pace Run — Maintain a steady pace over a distance. Tap to adjust your rhythm and keep your cadence in the green zone for the duration of the run.
💪
Rep Counter — Complete bodyweight reps at a controlled tempo. Hold proper form — too fast or too slow reduces your form score. Complete the set target.
💧
Hydration Check — Make the right hydration decisions at key moments during training. Choose when and what to drink to maintain performance.
🧘
Cool-Down Stretch — Hold each static stretch for the required duration. Progress through 5 stretches to bring your heart rate down and aid recovery.
