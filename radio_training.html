How to Play — Radio Comms

Mission

You are the duty operator at the signal tower. Remote stations call in with phonetic call signs (e.g. BRAVO-2, DELTA-4). Your job is to answer each one correctly using proper voice procedure.

How to Answer a Station

1. Click a station dot on the signal scope to select it. Its data block appears in the control panel.

2. Build a radio transmission by clicking chips in order: call sign → "this is CONTROL," → proword → confirm message.

3. Transmit. If the call sign, proword, and confirmation are correct, the station acknowledges and you score points.

Prowords

• ROGER — I have received and understood your message.

• WILCO — I will comply with your instructions.

• SAY AGAIN — Repeat your last transmission.

• RADIO CHECK — How do you read me?

• STANDBY — Wait, I will call you back.

Scoring

• Correct full call: +100 • Right call sign only: +30 • Correct proword: +20

• Wrong call sign: −20 • Station times out: −30 • Missing proword: −10

Watch Out

Each wave adds more stations and they arrive faster. The waveform at the bottom shows live audio — listen for the beeps and static. If a station waits too long it drops off the net. Keep the frequency clear and answer promptly!
