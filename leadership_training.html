▸ Command Overview

Leadership Fundamentals Command

Welcome to the Red Star Leadership Fundamentals training command. Your mission is to develop the leadership qualities expected of a second-year Army Cadet — influence, communication, problem solving, integrity, and teamwork. Based on PO 203 of the Red Star qualification standard (A-CR-CCP-702), you will work through eight leadership modules covering responsibilities of a Red Star cadet, principles of leadership, effective communication, positive group dynamics, influence behaviours, problem solving, personal integrity, and team-building. Complete training modules to earn XP, climb the rank ladder, and confirm your fundamentals in the Leadership Arena game.

Current Operation: Leadership Development Rotation

Your commanding officer has cleared your section to begin the Red Star Leadership Fundamentals training rotation. Work through each module in sequence — cadet responsibilities, principles of leadership, effective communication, positive group dynamics, influence behaviours, problem solving, personal integrity, and team-building.
